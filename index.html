About

01 / Who we are

Lujan is a design agency focusing on brand, accessibility, innovation, and empathy. Because design is everything.

We guide brands to best serve their clients with integrity, intentionality, and empathy.

Simply because we believe that good brands look out for their clients.

Founded in Garland and working across Dallas and the rest of Texas, Lujan builds brand systems, digital experiences and public art installations made to last.

We believe in accessibility you can feel, sites that load fast, and owning our tools. When big platforms squeeze creators, we build our own.

  • Brand

    Identities and visual systems that make complex ideas clear.

  • Accessibility

    Contrast, type, motion and keyboard access planned from the first sketch.

  • Innovation

    Creative technology, motion and generative work when the idea calls for it.

  • Empathy

    We start with the people a brand serves and design out from there.
